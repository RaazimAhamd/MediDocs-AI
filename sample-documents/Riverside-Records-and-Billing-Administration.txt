Riverside Community Hospital (Fictional)
Medical Records Requests and Billing Administration - Version 1.4
FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR SOFTWARE DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Riverside Community Hospital does not exist. This is not medical advice.

1. Requesting a copy of records
A patient may request a copy of their records by submitting Form MR-10 to the Health Information Office, in person or through the patient portal. A photo ID is required. Requests are processed within 10 business days of receipt of a complete form.

2. Copy fees
The first 20 pages of a paper copy are free. Additional pages are charged at USD 0.25 per page. Electronic copies delivered through the patient portal are always free.

3. Authorized representatives
A person who requests records on behalf of a patient must provide Form MR-12, signed by the patient, together with their own photo ID. Requests without a valid signed authorization are returned with an explanation.

4. Billing statements
Billing statements are issued monthly on the 5th day of the month. Payment is due within 30 days of the statement date. A reminder is sent if a payment has not been received after 45 days.

5. Payment plans
Patients with a balance above USD 200 may request a payment plan of up to 12 months. The minimum monthly payment is USD 25. Requests are made to Patient Accounts by telephone or through the patient portal.

6. Financial assistance
Patients who cannot pay in full may apply for financial assistance using Form FA-3 and proof of household income. A decision is made within 15 business days of receiving a complete application.

7. Billing disputes
Disputed charges must be reported to Patient Accounts in writing within 60 days of the statement date. The charge is placed on hold while it is reviewed.

8. Collections
An account may only be referred to an external collections agency after 120 days and after a final written notice has been sent.

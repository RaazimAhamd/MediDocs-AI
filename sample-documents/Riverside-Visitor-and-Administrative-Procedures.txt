Riverside Community Hospital (Fictional)
Visitor and Administrative Procedures - Version 2.1
FICTIONAL DOCUMENT FOR SOFTWARE DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Riverside Community Hospital does not exist. This is not medical advice.

1. Visiting hours
General wards are open to visitors every day from 10:00 to 20:00. Intensive Care Unit (ICU) visiting hours are 14:00 to 16:00 and 19:00 to 20:00. In the ICU no more than 2 visitors may be at the bedside at the same time, and each visit is limited to 20 minutes. In the maternity ward, one designated support person may stay overnight.

2. Visitor registration and badges
All visitors must register at the front desk and show a photo ID. A visitor badge is issued for the day and must be worn visibly at all times. Badges must be returned at the front desk when leaving. Visitors without a badge may be asked to leave clinical areas.

3. Children and groups
Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult who is not the patient. Children under 12 are not permitted in the ICU unless the ward manager approves it in advance. Groups of more than 4 visitors are asked to take turns in the waiting area.

4. Health precautions for visitors
Visitors who have had a fever, cough or diarrhoea in the past 48 hours are asked to postpone their visit until they have been symptom-free for 48 hours. Hand sanitiser stations are located at every ward entrance, and visitors are asked to use them on entry and exit.

5. Photography and recording
Photography, filming and audio recording are not allowed in clinical areas unless the Communications Office has given written permission. Staff may ask visitors to delete images taken without permission.

6. Parking
The first 30 minutes of parking are free. Visitors can have their parking ticket validated at the front desk for a flat rate of USD 5 per day.

7. Lost property
Items found on the premises are held at the Security Office for 30 days. After 30 days unclaimed items are donated or securely destroyed.

8. Complaints and feedback
Complaints can be submitted to Patient Relations in person, by telephone or through the patient portal within 90 days of the incident. Patient Relations acknowledges every complaint within 3 business days and aims to resolve it within 20 business days. If a complainant is unhappy with the outcome they may request a review by the Quality Committee.
